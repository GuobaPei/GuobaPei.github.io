I want to build physical intelligence you can trust outside the lab — models that still hold up when the real world shifts scale, degrades, or hands them something they have never seen. I chase that along three threads: neural operators that let science compute what it could not before, world models for autonomous driving, and multimodal models that act reliably in the world instead of only on a benchmark.
